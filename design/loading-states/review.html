Missing loading states — review sheet

Thirteen routes have a page.tsx and no loading.tsx. They all fall back to app/loading.tsx: three grey cards in a centred max-w-5xl main. On an auth page or the brands grid that is the wrong shape, so the page jumps when it resolves.

Ten of the thirteen need nothing new. Three screens cover all thirteen, and two of the three reuse a skeleton that already exists in the repo. The table below says which route gets which.

Three screens, not thirteen. The brief listed thirteen routes. Ten of them either share one shell (the auth group), never render (the /admin redirect), or already hold their own space (/onboarding/manual, /sso-callback). Writing thirteen near-identical files would be thirteen things to keep in step with the pages they stand in for.

Nothing new was invented. The skeleton primitives are the ones S00a and S00b were approved with: one grey (#e8ecf2), one paler grey for secondary copy, a 1.8s pulse, animation off under prefers-reduced-motion. LD-1 draws the shape of the existing AuthFormSkeleton; LD-3's bar is the shape of the existing OnboardingHeaderSkeleton; the frames, the panel ground, the method cards and the brand card box are the approved AUTH-1, AUTH-7 and BA-1 CSS, imported, not restated.

Static chrome stays, only the request-dependent part is sketched. That is the rule the 27 existing loading.tsx files already follow — app/(main)/admin/(agency)/clients/loading.tsx renders the real PageHeader with its real title and description and sketches only the rows. Both new main-area screens do the same.

What this does not fix. app/loading.tsx at the root is still a generic three-card placeholder with a leftover ClientsLoading name. Once LD-1 and LD-3 are wired, the only routes left under it are the ones that never show it, so it could be deleted or reduced to a bare frame — a separate call, and a code change, so it is not drawn here.
