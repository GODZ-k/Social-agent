# Worked examples by business type

Six business types the outline asked for, each worked from the diagnosis to the `growthLever` sentence, with what social cannot do written as `constraints` or `openQuestions`. They are patterns, not answers: a real brief cites this business's reviews, prices and questionnaire, never a pattern. Figures are illustrative, in the business's own currency.

## Bakery

**Diagnosis.** Walk-in, one site, sells out of the popular lines by 11 am, quiet after 2 pm, 80 reviews at 4.8, no pre-order. Owner's `goal` `bigger_orders`. Bottleneck `orderValue` (full in the morning, and nothing on the site invites a larger purchase: no whole cakes to order, no boxes, no pre-order).

**`growthLever`.** "Use the making of whole cakes and celebration boxes, priced and ordered by the pre-order form the owner is adding, to move regulars within walking distance from a $5 pastry to a $40 order, so that pre-orders and afternoon sales rise."

**Social cannot.** Sell more of what sells out by 11 am (`constraints`: "the morning lines are capped; posts about them only lengthen the queue"). Take the order until the form exists (`openQuestions`: "When will pre-orders be live, and how many whole cakes a week can you make?").

## Clinic (physio, skin, dental)

**Diagnosis.** Bookings, high-consideration, treatments from $80 to $3,000, 30 reviews at 4.3, no prices or faces on the site, phone booking only. Owner's `goal` `more_customers`. Bottleneck `trust`, with a `conversion` break behind it.

**`growthLever`.** "Use the practitioners explaining one condition or treatment at a time, with consented results and stated prices, to make adults within a 30-minute drive trust the clinic before their first visit, so that new-patient bookings for the named treatment rise."

**Social cannot.** Book the appointment while booking is phone-only (`constraints`: "until online booking exists, posts direct people to call in hours or DM; response time must be under a working day"). Publish results without consent (`openQuestions`: "Which practitioners will appear on camera, and do you hold consent for any before-and-after cases?"). Fill a diary that is already full (`openQuestions`: "How many new-patient slots a week are open?").

## Salon

**Diagnosis.** Bookings, four chairs, Tuesdays and Wednesdays half empty, Saturdays booked three weeks out, 210 reviews at 4.9, online booking works. Owner's `goal` `more_customers`. Bottleneck `repeat` for the midweek slots (regulars fill midweek; strangers fill Saturdays, which are full).

**`growthLever`.** "Use each stylist's work on real clients, with rebooking reminders on a six-to-eight-week cycle and a midweek slot the owner prices, to bring existing clients within 15 minutes back into Tuesday and Wednesday, so that midweek chair fill rises."

**Social cannot.** Add Saturday capacity (`constraints`: "Saturday is full; content that brings strangers only lengthens the wait"). Run the midweek offer without the owner pricing it (`openQuestions`: "What will the midweek slot cost, and will the booking software send reminders?").

## Consultant

**Diagnosis.** Enquiry-to-quote, sells to finance directors of firms with 20 to 200 staff, three to five clients a year at $10,000 or more each, no case studies on the site, a contact form. Platforms chosen: linkedin, instagram. Bottleneck `trust`.

**`growthLever`.** "Use the consultant explaining one client problem and its outcome at a time, with named clients where permitted, to make finance directors of mid-sized firms trust the work before a call, so that qualified enquiries through LinkedIn and the form rise to one a month."

**Social cannot.** Reach this buyer on Instagram (`constraints`: "the buyer is on LinkedIn; Instagram is at most a credibility check when they look the consultant up"). Replace outreach (`constraints`: "at three to five clients a year, direct outreach and referrals do most of the work; posts support them"). Use client names without permission (`openQuestions`: "Which past clients can be named, and which outcomes can be stated in numbers?").

## Online shop

**Diagnosis.** Online orders, skincare, ships nationally, average order unknown, 400 reviews at 4.6 on the product pages, one hero product with a 6-week rebuy cycle, no subscribe option, email list of unknown size. Owner's `goal` `repeat_customers`. Bottleneck `repeat`.

**`growthLever`.** "Use the hero product's six-week cycle, with routine and pairing content and a subscribe-and-save option the owner adds, to bring first-time buyers anywhere in the country back for a second order, so that the repeat-order rate rises."

**Social cannot.** Reach past buyers reliably through the feed (`constraints`: "organic feed reach to followers is small as of 2026-09; the repeat lever runs on email, SMS or broadcast channels, with posts as the reason"). Offer subscribe-and-save before it exists (`openQuestions`: "Will you add a subscription option, and what is the current repeat rate and email list size?").

## Restaurant

**Diagnosis.** Bookings plus walk-in, 60 covers, weekends full, Monday to Wednesday at 40 percent, reservation widget works, 300 reviews at 4.5 with a recurring "slow service on Saturday" theme, no set menu or events. Owner's `goal` `more_customers`. Bottleneck `repeat` for midweek, with a service issue on Saturday that social cannot touch.

**`growthLever`.** "Use the kitchen and the weekly midweek dish or set menu the owner creates, shown as it is made and bookable in one tap, to bring people who have already eaten here back on Monday to Wednesday, so that midweek covers rise from 40 percent toward weekend levels."

**Social cannot.** Fix Saturday service (`constraints`: "the recurring 'slow on Saturday' reviews are a floor issue; posts that bring more Saturday bookings would make it worse until it is fixed"). Fill weekends that are full (`constraints`: "weekend content is proof for midweek, not a call to book"). Create the midweek menu (`openQuestions`: "Will you run a midweek set menu or event, and at what price?").
