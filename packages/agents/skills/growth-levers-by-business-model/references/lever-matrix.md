# Lever matrix

The six levers of `growth-levers-by-business-model`, and for each: which bottleneck mechanism applies, what content moves it, the business KPI it feeds (for `kpi-selection`), where the path usually breaks, and the platform notes as of 2026-09. Platform behaviour changes; re-check the dated notes before relying on them.

## Local footfall

| | |
|---|---|
| Counts as | people through the door, covers, tickets at the till |
| Catchment | 1 to 2 km for a daily-visit business; a town for a destination (weekend brunch, a specialist shop) |
| Bottleneck mechanisms | `awareness`: location-tagged short video, local collaborations, "you have walked past this" content; `repeat`: what is new this week, regulars, a counter-run offer; `orderValue`: pairings, the bigger size, the thing beside the till |
| Content that moves it | the product being made, the room at its best time, the people behind the counter, the weekly special, what to order with what, the street and the neighbours |
| KPI it feeds | covers or transactions per day, split by the slow period named in `capacity`; new vs returning if the till or a loyalty scheme can tell them apart |
| Where it breaks | hours or address wrong or missing on the profile; the post shows a thing not on the menu that week; the offer needs staff to know about it |
| Platform notes (2026-09) | Instagram Reels and TikTok reach non-followers by recommendation and can be location-tagged; Facebook reaches locals through groups and events; LinkedIn is irrelevant for footfall except for a lunch trade near offices |

## Bookings

| | |
|---|---|
| Counts as | appointments or reservations made and kept |
| Catchment | the drive time people accept for the service: 15 minutes for a haircut, an hour for a specialist |
| Bottleneck mechanisms | `trust`: the person doing the work, the process, results with consent, prices; `conversion`: one tap to a bookable slot, the price beside it; `orderValue`: the add-on offered at booking, the premium time, the treatment bundle; `repeat`: rebooking reminders on the service's cycle |
| Content that moves it | a named practitioner explaining one thing, a walk through what happens at the first appointment, a result with the customer's permission, "this is what it costs", the empty slot this week |
| KPI it feeds | bookings per week, new-client bookings, the fill rate of the slow days, value per booking when the owner gives `orderValue` |
| Where it breaks | phone-only booking; a booking page that hides prices or asks for an account; the practitioner in the post is not the one available; the slot in the post is gone by the time it is read |
| Platform notes (2026-09) | Instagram profile links and DMs are where most local booking enquiries start; Facebook carries older customers and recommendations in groups; TikTok reaches under-30s and travels beyond the catchment, which is waste for a one-chair salon |

## Online orders

| | |
|---|---|
| Counts as | carts checked out, first orders, repeat orders |
| Catchment | wherever the business ships or delivers; the only lever where wide reach is not waste |
| Bottleneck mechanisms | `awareness`: the product in use, shareable and saveable formats, creators with the same customer; `trust`: reviews with source, the making, the founder, the returns policy; `conversion`: the product page linked directly, the price in the post, shipping said up front; `repeat`: the rebuy cycle, the subscribe option, what to pair; `orderValue`: bundles, the free-shipping threshold, gift sets |
| Content that moves it | the product doing its job, unboxing by a customer, before and after, the ingredient or material story, the founder answering the top objection, "which one is for me" |
| KPI it feeds | orders per week, average order value, repeat-order rate, revenue when the owner shares it |
| Where it breaks | the link goes to the home page; shipping cost appears at checkout; the checkout demands an account; a product in the post is out of stock |
| Platform notes (2026-09) | Instagram and TikTok both have in-app shops and product tags; TikTok Shop conversion depends on the category and country, so check availability; Facebook and Instagram share a catalogue; LinkedIn is only for B2B products |

## Leads

| | |
|---|---|
| Counts as | enquiries, quote requests, calls, DMs the owner can quote |
| Catchment | the service area (trades), a sector (consultants, agencies), a region (B2B) |
| Bottleneck mechanisms | `trust`: finished work walked through, the owner explaining cost and process, named clients with permission, credentials; `conversion`: the form cut to three fields, a stated response time, "from" prices; `awareness`: being remembered before the need arises, local groups, referrers |
| Content that moves it | the last job, what went wrong and how it was fixed, "what this costs and why", the questions people ask before buying, a client's own words, the owner on camera |
| KPI it feeds | qualified enquiries per month, quote-to-win rate when the owner tracks it, value of jobs won |
| Where it breaks | nobody replies within a working day; the form is long; the post promises a service the owner cannot take on now; urgent-intent customers who search rather than scroll are chased with posts |
| Platform notes (2026-09) | Facebook local groups and recommendations still produce trade leads; Instagram works for visual trades (bathrooms, gardens, joinery); LinkedIn is the only reliable place for B2B and professional-service leads; TikTok reach is broad and mostly outside the service area |

## Repeat customers

| | |
|---|---|
| Counts as | second and later purchases per customer; share of a week's customers who have been before |
| Catchment | existing customers only; reach beyond them is a different lever |
| Bottleneck mechanisms | `repeat` only: a rhythm (weekly, monthly), a reason (what is new, the seasonal rotation), a mechanism the owner runs off-platform (loyalty, a list, reminders) |
| Content that moves it | this week's thing, the regulars (with permission), the seasonal change, the loyalty count, "you are due", the behind-the-scenes that makes people feel part of it |
| KPI it feeds | returning share, visits per regular per month, loyalty sign-ups, list size and open rate when a list exists |
| Where it breaks | no mechanism exists, so returns cannot be counted; the rebuy cycle is longer than a month (then this is not the lever); the content is only for strangers |
| Platform notes (2026-09) | organic reach to existing followers on Instagram and Facebook is a small fraction of followers per post, so a repeat lever leans on Stories, broadcast channels, DMs, WhatsApp or email, not the feed alone |

## Hiring

| | |
|---|---|
| Counts as | applications from people worth interviewing |
| Catchment | commuting distance |
| Bottleneck mechanisms | `trust` and `awareness` among candidates: what it is like to work there, the people, the pay and hours stated plainly |
| Content that moves it | a day in the role, the team, what the job pays and when the shifts are, the owner saying why the place is worth working at |
| KPI it feeds | applications per opening, hires made |
| Where it breaks | no stated pay or hours; the application route is "message us"; the content is customer-facing only |
| Platform notes (2026-09) | Facebook and LinkedIn for adults with experience; Instagram and TikTok for under-25s in hospitality and retail |

Only when the owner says staff is the cap on growth. A café that cannot open on Sundays for lack of staff has a hiring lever, and that is the growth brief's honest answer even though the owner asked for more customers.
