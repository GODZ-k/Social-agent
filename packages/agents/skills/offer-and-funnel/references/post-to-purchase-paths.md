# Post-to-purchase paths

The path from a post to a paid customer for each lever, the step where it usually breaks, what to write into `constraints` when it does, and how to walk it with one `read-page`. Platform mechanics are as of 2026-09; re-check the notes marked so.

## The general path

1. **Post.** Seen in a feed, mostly by non-followers on Instagram Reels and TikTok, mostly by followers and group members on Facebook (2026-09).
2. **Profile or link.** One tap to the profile; one more to the link in bio or the link sticker. On Instagram, only Stories and the bio carry tappable links; feed captions do not (2026-09). Each tap loses a share of the people who took the last one.
3. **Landing page.** The page the link opens. It should be the offer's page, not the home page.
4. **Action.** Book, order, call, DM, walk in.
5. **Confirmation.** Something that tells the customer it worked: a booking email, an order number, a reply.
6. **Visit or delivery.** The money changes hands, or the customer turns up.

Walking it: open the landing page with `read-page` and check three things: is the price there, is the action one tap away, and does the page match what the post would promise. Report what you found in the offer's `why` or in `constraints`.

## Local footfall

**Path.** Post, then the address and hours on the profile, then the visit. No landing page needed.

**Breaks.** Hours or address wrong or missing on the profile; the post shows a product not available that week; a counter offer staff do not know about.

**Constraint wording.** "The profile must show current hours and the address; the weekly special must be on the counter the day it is posted."

**Walk it.** Read the site's contact or hours page and compare with what the scan found. Check that the item in the questionnaire `bestSellers` is on the menu page.

## Bookings

**Path.** Post, then the profile link or DM, then the booking page, then a slot chosen and confirmed, then the appointment.

**Breaks.** Phone-only booking in working hours; a booking page with no prices; a page that asks for an account before showing availability; the slot in the post gone by the time it is read; the practitioner in the post not the one available.

**Constraint wording.** "Booking is phone-only in working hours; until online booking exists, posts point to DM and the owner replies within one working day." Or: "The booking page must show prices for [service] before posts about it run."

**Walk it.** Read the booking page. Note whether a price and a slot are visible without logging in.

## Online orders

**Path.** Post, then the product link (bio, Story sticker, product tag), then the product page, then add to cart, then checkout, then delivery.

**Breaks.** Link to the home page instead of the product; shipping cost or delivery area only shown at checkout; checkout demands an account; the product is out of stock; the price in the post differs from the page.

**Constraint wording.** "Every product post links to that product's page; shipping cost and area must be on the product page, not only at checkout."

**Walk it.** Read the product page of the first priority offer. Note price, stock, shipping information and whether the buy button is above the fold.

## Leads

**Path.** Post, then profile, then the form, phone or DM, then a reply, then a quote or call, then the job.

**Breaks.** A form asking more than a name, a contact and the need; no stated response time; nobody replies within a working day; the post promises a service the owner cannot take on now; the enquiry lands in an inbox nobody checks.

**Constraint wording.** "Enquiries land in the form and the DMs; the owner names who replies and commits to one working day, or the lead is lost." Or: "The form should ask three things: name, contact, what you need."

**Walk it.** Read the contact or quote page. Count the form fields. Check for a stated response time and a phone number.

## Repeat customers

**Path.** Post or Story or broadcast message, then the reminder or reason, then the return visit or reorder.

**Breaks.** No mechanism exists, so there is no list to send to and no way to count returns; the feed alone is relied on, and organic reach to followers is small (2026-09); the rhythm slips.

**Constraint wording.** "No list or loyalty mechanism exists; posts can invite returns but nothing tracks them until the owner starts one." Or: "The repeat lever runs on the WhatsApp list the owner keeps; posts give the reason, the list carries the reminder."

**Walk it.** Check the site for a newsletter sign-up, loyalty page or app link. If none, it is an open question, not a break to fix in the posts.

## What a two-tap loss looks like

No exact figure is sourced here, and none should be written into a brief. The rule that holds: every extra tap loses a share of the people who took the last one, so a link to the offer's page beats a link to the home page, and a DM beats a five-field form when the owner replies quickly. State the principle and the specific fix, not a percentage.
