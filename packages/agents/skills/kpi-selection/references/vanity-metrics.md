# Vanity metrics and how to talk an owner out of them

As of 2026-09. The owner usually asks for one of these because it is the number they can see. Do not refuse it; demote it to a tier-3 indicator with a baseline target, and put a business result above it. The lines below are the argument, in the owner's terms.

| Metric asked for | Why the owner wants it | What it hides | What to say | Keep as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Followers | visible on the profile; competitors have more | who they are and where; 2,000 followers in another city buy nothing; bought or lapsed followers cost reach | "A follower who never comes in is a cost, not an asset. We want the 300 who live within ten minutes. We will count follows, but the number that pays the rent is X." | leading indicator, baseline in month 1, local follows preferred |
| Likes | the fastest feedback there is | a like is a reflex; it predicts nothing about a visit; the platforms weight saves, shares and watch time far more than likes (Meta and TikTok statements 2023-2025) | "A like is a nod. A save is a plan. We will watch saves and shares, and count what you can count: bookings." | not a KPI; report it only as part of engagement |
| Reach or impressions | it sounds like advertising exposure | reach to the wrong people is free and worthless; a viral post about a cat reaches people who will never buy a boiler; impressions count the same person many times | "Reach matters only if the bottleneck is that nobody local knows you. Is it?" Ask; if yes, keep local reach; if no, drop it | leading indicator only for `awareness` |
| Video views | big numbers, fast | a view is counted at 1-3 seconds on most platforms (as of 2026-09; verify); views from outside the catchment do not visit | "Half a second of attention from a stranger is not a customer. We will watch how many people watched to the end and then visited the profile." | not a KPI; use profile visits after video |
| Engagement rate | it looks like a professional metric | it falls as followers grow and rises when the account is tiny; nobody agrees on the formula (by followers or by reach) | "It is a decent read on whether people who see us care. It is not a result. We will report it, not target it." | leading indicator for `trust`, no rate target |
| Going viral | one post could change everything | it happens to posts that are not about the business, to an audience that is not the customer; it does not repeat | "A viral post is weather. We are building a climate: the same 500 locals seeing you three times a week." | never a KPI |
| Posting frequency | it feels like effort made | posts nobody needed; frequency is an input the strategy sets, not a result | "We will post on a schedule. Frequency is our promise to you, not a result for you." | constraint or plan item, not a KPI |
| Hashtag ranking, "top post" badges | visible proof of winning | hashtag ranking has had little effect on reach on Instagram since 2023-2024 (as of 2026-09; verify); the badge is for other creators | "That rank is for people who post, not for people who buy." | drop |
| Website traffic (as the only number) | it is measurable and moves with posts | traffic without an enquiry or an order is a tour; social traffic often bounces in seconds | "Traffic is fine as a signal. What did those visitors do? We will count the form." | tier-2 only when the site has a conversion path |

## The one-paragraph version for the brief's `why`

When demoting a vanity metric, write the reason in the KPI's `why` rather than in a separate note, so the owner reads it where the number appears. Example: `{ name: "Local follows per month", target: "Baseline in the first 4 weeks after connection.", why: "You asked for followers; we count follows from accounts within the catchment, because a follower who cannot visit does not move bookings. The result above is what follows should lead to." }`

## When the owner insists

Keep their metric in the list, set its target to a baseline, and put the tier-1 result above it. Record the disagreement in `openQuestions` as a question ("Should the monthly report lead with followers or with bookings?") so the Performance Analyst reports both and the numbers settle it.
