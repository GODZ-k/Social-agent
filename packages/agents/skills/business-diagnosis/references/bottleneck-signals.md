# Bottleneck signals

The full table behind Step 2 of `business-diagnosis`. For each `bottleneck.kind`: what counts as evidence (citable in `why`), what is only a hunch (say so, or ask), the counter-signals that point elsewhere, and the question to ask when the evidence is thin. Numbers are rough and as of 2026-09; re-check them when platforms change what they show.

## Reading order

Walk the stages in order (awareness, trust, conversion, repeat, orderValue) and stop at the first stage with strong evidence of a break. Then check the counter-signals for that stage; if two or more counter-signals hold, move on to the next stage. A business with strong evidence at two stages gets the earlier stage unless the later one unlocks more revenue with the capacity that is known.

## awareness

| Evidence (citable) | Hunch only | Counter-signals | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Absent from the first page of a category-plus-town search | "The area is competitive" | Over about 100 Google reviews | "Where do most new customers hear about you today?" |
| Under about 20 Google reviews, or none | A small Instagram following on its own (many good local businesses have one) | Ranks top three for its category in its town | "How many people a week ask how they found you?" |
| Trading under 2 years, or a new location | | The owner says they are busy or turning people away | |
| One location in a town with many similar businesses | | | |
| Owner's `goal` is `awareness` or `launch` and the site has no visible friction | | | |

Typical for: new cafés, second locations, any business that moved or renamed, businesses that relied on one referral source.

## trust

| Evidence (citable) | Hunch only | Counter-signals | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| High-consideration purchase (over about $150, health, home, money, children, pets) and the site shows no proof: no reviews, no faces, no credentials, no results, no prices | "The design looks dated" | Reviews over 4.5 with over about 50 ratings | "Can the owner or staff appear on camera?" |
| Rating under about 4.2 with recurring complaints on the same theme | | Competitors also hide prices and faces (then it is the category norm, not this business's break) | "Do you have consented before-and-after cases or named testimonials?" |
| Complaints unanswered for more than a month | | Site has a full price list, team photos and case studies | "Who replies to reviews today?" |
| Competitors show prices, faces or results and this business does not | | | |
| Copy is all superlatives with no counts, years, names or awards | | | |

Typical for: clinics, dentists, trades doing big jobs, consultants, wedding suppliers, anything with a deposit.

## conversion

| Evidence (citable) | Hunch only | Counter-signals | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booking or ordering only by phone in working hours | "The site feels slow" | Online booking or cart, working on a phone, with prices | "How many enquiries a week do you get, and how many become customers?" |
| No prices on a service that at least two competitors price openly | | A clear primary call to action on every page | "What happens to an enquiry after 6 pm?" |
| Menu, price list or brochure only as a PDF | | | |
| A form asking more than a name, a contact and the one thing wanted | | | |
| Primary call to action is "enquire" or "contact us" for something that could be booked | | | |
| Cart requires an account, or shipping is only shown at the end | | | |
| Reviews or questionnaire mention "hard to book", "never answered", "waited weeks" | | | |

Typical for: salons and clinics still on phone booking, restaurants with no reservation link, shops whose web store is an afterthought.

## repeat

| Evidence (citable) | Hunch only | Counter-signals | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product is naturally rebought monthly or more (food, drink, hair, nails, cleaning, pet care, dental hygiene, gym) and nothing on the site or in the questionnaire brings people back: no loyalty, no list, no reminders, no regulars' content | "Customers probably do not come back" | The owner says most customers are regulars | "Do you have any list of past customers (email, WhatsApp, booking software)?" |
| Owner's `goal` is `repeat_customers` | | A loyalty scheme, subscription or reminder system exists and is used | "Roughly what share of a week's customers have been before?" |
| Owner's `capacity` names slow days or hours that regulars would fill | | Product is bought once (a wedding, a bathroom, a course) | "Do you run any loyalty or referral scheme?" |
| Competitors nearby run loyalty and this business does not | | | |

Typical for: cafés, bakeries, salons, barbers, takeaways, pet groomers, dental hygiene, gyms.

## orderValue

| Evidence (citable) | Hunch only | Counter-signals | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner's `capacity` or the site says fully booked, waiting list, queues, "book weeks ahead" | "They could charge more" | Slow days named in `capacity` | "What is a typical ticket, and what is the biggest thing a customer can buy?" |
| Owner's `goal` is `bigger_orders` | | Reviews mention price as a complaint | "Which items or services carry the best margin?" |
| Price list has no add-on, bundle, premium tier or larger size | | Owner wants volume and has room | "Could staff offer an add-on at the point of sale?" |
| `bestSellers` names a high-margin item that is not featured on the site | | | |
| Owner's `orderValue` is low relative to the visible price list (many small tickets) | | | |

Typical for: busy cafés and restaurants, salons at capacity, trades with a full diary, popular studios.

## When two stages tie

- `awareness` vs `trust`: trust when the purchase is high-consideration and the proof is missing; awareness when the proof is there and nobody finds it.
- `trust` vs `conversion`: conversion when the proof is on the site but the action is hard; trust when the action is easy and people still do not take it.
- `repeat` vs `orderValue`: repeat when there are slow periods to fill; orderValue when the business is full at all times.
- Any stage vs `awareness` for a launch: awareness for the launch window, and the brief names the stage the launch will run into next.
