# Platforms by segment

As of 2026-09. These are category and age patterns to fall back on when the brand kit and the competitor notes say nothing; they do not raise a segment's `basis`. Re-check yearly: platform audiences drift, and a pattern from 2024 may be wrong by 2027. `platforms` only takes `instagram`, `facebook`, `linkedin`, `tiktok`.

## What each platform is, for a small business

| Platform | Who a small business reaches there (broad pattern, 2024-2026 industry surveys) | What lands | Weak for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `instagram` | adults roughly 18-45; local discovery through the map, hashtags of the town and Reels; food, beauty, fitness, retail and home categories | short video, before-and-after, the product in the hand, the person behind the counter, a saved-worthy menu or price list | text-heavy explanations; B2B procurement |
| `facebook` | adults roughly 30-65 and local community groups; parents, homeowners, trades' customers, older patients; event RSVPs and "recommend a plumber" threads | plain photos with a sentence, events, opening-hour changes, reviews shared, answers in local groups | under-25s; anything that needs to look designed |
| `tiktok` | roughly 16-35 and rising older; discovery by interest not by follow, so a local business can reach people outside its town who cannot buy | a person talking to camera, a process from start to finish, a price reveal, humour that is actually in the brand's voice | a strictly local catchment; a brand whose voice is formal |
| `linkedin` | working adults in their job role; B2B buyers, professionals, recruiters; local business owners who buy from each other | a named person's opinion, a case with a number, a short lesson from a job | consumers buying for themselves; walk-in trade |

## Defaults by segment type

Use the first platform when only one is allowed; the second when the brand already posts there or the competitor notes point to it.

| Segment type | Default | Second | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local adult buying for themselves (coffee, haircut, gym, dentist) | `instagram` | `facebook` | local discovery on Instagram; Facebook for the over-40s and for the review threads |
| Parent buying for a child (classes, dentist, tutor, party) | `facebook` | `instagram` | parents' local groups are on Facebook; recommendations are asked there |
| Homeowner with an emergency (plumber, electrician, locksmith) | `facebook` | none | the "anyone know a plumber" thread; Instagram is not opened at 11pm with a leak |
| Homeowner planning a project (kitchen, garden, extension) | `instagram` | `facebook` | saves of before-and-after; Facebook groups for quotes |
| Landlord, letting agent, office manager (B2B-ish local service) | `linkedin` | `facebook` | buys in their job role; Facebook only for small landlords |
| Under-25 consumer (fashion, food trends, beauty) | `tiktok` | `instagram` | discovery by interest; Instagram for the follow after discovery |
| Online shopper, 25-45, considered purchase (skincare, gifts, home) | `instagram` | `tiktok` | product in use and reviews; TikTok for reach beyond followers |
| Professional buying a service for their company (agency, consultant, software) | `linkedin` | none | the only platform where the job role is the audience |
| Older patient or customer, 55+ (clinic, hearing, mobility, garden centre) | `facebook` | none | the age pattern; Instagram use falls off above 55 in most surveys |
| Tourist or visitor (restaurant, attraction, B&B) | `instagram` | `tiktok` | discovery by place; saves before a trip |

## Rules

- One platform is a complete answer. Two is the maximum for a small business that has to make the content; three or four in a segment means the researcher has not decided.
- A brand that posts nowhere yet gets the default; say in `summary` that the platform is a category default.
- A brand's `constraints` (questionnaire) can rule a platform out ("no TikTok, we are a law firm"); obey it.
- A segment whose competitors' customers demonstrably appear on one platform (a competitor note that says "reviews reference their Facebook group") takes that platform over the default.
- Do not choose a platform for its reach. Reach outside the catchment is not a reason for a local business.

## Things that change, to re-check

- Meta's age distribution on Instagram and Facebook (surveys 2024-2026 put Facebook's median user older each year).
- TikTok's local-discovery features and whether search by place is common in the brand's country.
- Whether the brand's country has a strong local-group culture on Facebook (the UK, Ireland, Australia, much of Europe: yes; some markets prefer WhatsApp communities, which the schema does not cover).
